About MEVCO

A local foundation. A global outlook.

An independent Lebanese business created to connect international consumer brands with local retail opportunities.

Our story

Commercial experience meets a focused new ambition.

MEVCO International is being established by entrepreneurs with business interests spanning industrial trading, healthcare retail, hospitality and fashion.

Those experiences inform our understanding of customer relationships, everyday operations and the importance of building a trusted reputation.

MEVCO is a separate venture with its own strategy: to develop a select portfolio of quality international consumer health and personal-care brands for the Lebanese market.
